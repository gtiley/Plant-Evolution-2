The lecuture meets two times a week from 10:40-12:30 on Tuesday and Thursday (4 credit hours). Lectures will take place on Tuesdays and will mostly be Socratic, building directly from reference materials. Discussions and labs on Thursdays will draw from current literature and require active participation. There will be one near-campus field trips that will require additional time outside of class. There are a number of required journal articles that should be read for class. They will typically be written beyond the scope of class materials at the time, so you will be expected to be, in part, confused. Perfection is not expected or encouraged in this class.
